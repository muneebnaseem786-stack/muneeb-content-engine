## 1. RELEVANCE (1-5)
Does the content engage with the ACTUAL argument or claim in the article/note,
or does it talk about an adjacent / unrelated topic? Substack readers expect
you to have read the piece — drift is obvious.
- 5: addresses a specific claim, framework, or example from the piece

- 1: ignores the piece, generic finance commentary

## 2. VOICE MATCH (1-5)
Does it sound like Muneeb's Substack voice — first-person, conversational,
analytical?
- 5: reads like Muneeb thinking out loud after reading the piece
- 3: third-person analytical, no personal voice
- 1: corporate / motivational / Aakash-X-punchy / generic LinkedIn voice

## 3. COMPLIANCE — FAIL if ANY apply
- **FORCED MENA / UAE / GULF ANGLE**: injects Gulf/UAE/Pakistan perspective
