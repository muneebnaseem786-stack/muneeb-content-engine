# Platform: eToro (Popular Investor Program)

## Overview
eToro is a trading and social investment platform. The **Popular Investor Program (PIP)** pays creators a share of fees + base payments based on copier count and AUM. The strategic frame for this account is **not "start an eToro venture"** — it is **add eToro as a third distribution endpoint** for the markets/portfolio content already being produced for X and Substack, with the upside of meaningful passive income at Elite/Diamond tier.

## How the Popular Investor Program Works

### Tier Progression & Economics
| Tier | Requirements (approx.) | Monthly Pay |
|------|------------------------|-------------|
| Cadet | Verified profile, ID, public username | ~$500 base |
| Champion | $50K AUM, 10+ copiers, 2+ months | ~$1K base + spread share |
| Elite | $500K AUM, 1+ year track | Base + 1.5% AUM/yr |
| Elite Pro | $1M AUM | 1.5% + spread |
| Diamond | $5M+ AUM | 2% AUM/yr + spread (six figures realistic) |

**The economic reality:** Cadet money is symbolic. Elite is where the math starts to matter ($500K AUM × 1.5% = $7,500/yr). Diamond is the actual prize. Plan for 18-24 months minimum to reach Elite if execution is consistent.

### What Drives Copier Growth
1. **Posting consistency** — silence kills the algorithm and trust simultaneously
2. **Low Risk Score** — copiers filter aggressively at Risk Score 4 or below; anything above 6 cuts the addressable copier pool in half
3. **Low drawdown** — copiers tolerate slower returns but flee at -20%+ drawdowns
4. **Win rate clarity** — eToro displays this prominently; 50%+ win rate with positive R is the threshold
5. **Narrative around the numbers** — copiers copy *people*, not P&L. Without posts, you are a number on a leaderboard.

### Strategy Distortion Risk (Important)
Once you have copiers, every trade has audience implications. The danger: trading to manage Risk Score instead of for actual return. Decide upfront which strategy decisions are non-negotiable and which can flex for copier optics. Examples:
- Non-negotiable: thesis-driven holdings, position sizing rules
- Flex-allowed: leverage usage, position concentration above 20%, frequency of trades

## Content Pillars

Four content types, each with a different cost profile. Mix matters more than volume.

### Pillar 1 — Position Thesis (Why I Own X)
- **Purpose:** Builds the deepest copier trust. Each thesis post is evergreen — works months after publication.
- **Trigger:** New position OR re-articulating a long-held position when sentiment shifts.
- **Cost:** High (real work — competitive moat, bull case, bear case, sizing rationale).
- **Cadence:** Once per holding, then refreshed annually or on thesis-changing event.
- **Format:** 200-400 words. Bull case, bear case, why I'm sized as I am, what would make me sell. Specific numbers.

### Pillar 2 — Earnings Reaction (Held Into Print)
- **Purpose:** Highest engagement type. Earnings days are when copiers panic — your post is the anchor.
- **Trigger:** Earnings release on a held position.
- **Cost:** Medium, but time-locked (post within 4-12 hours of release).
- **Cadence:** Every earnings on a held name (typically 4-8 events per quarter depending on holdings).
- **Format:** Headline number reaction → what mattered vs. what didn't → did this change the thesis → action (hold/add/trim/exit) with reasoning.

### Pillar 3 — Strategy & Framework Posts
- **Purpose:** Builds trust by disclosing *how* you trade, not just *what*. Copiers want to predict you.
- **Trigger:** Reflective — drawdown periods, position sizing changes, sell rule activation.
- **Cost:** Medium (reflective, not researched).
- **Cadence:** 1-2x per month.
- **Format:** One principle per post. Concrete example from your own portfolio.

### Pillar 4 — Macro/Markets (Repurposed from X)
- **Purpose:** Keeps the feed alive between earnings + position events. Near-zero marginal cost.
- **Trigger:** Whenever the markets-post skill fires on X.
- **Cost:** Low (reformatting only).
- **Cadence:** 1x/week filler.
- **Format:** Trim X version to remove platform-specific phrasing. Add "implication for my portfolio" sentence — this is the eToro-specific twist that makes it not just copy-paste.

## Cadence Rules

| Period | Target | Floor |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Normal weeks | 2 posts | 1 post |
| Earnings season (4 wks/qtr) | 3 posts | 2 posts |
| Major macro event week | +1 reactive post | — |

**Consistency rule:** 1.5x/week sustained beats 3x/week with month-long gaps. If a week is impossible, post 1 macro repurpose at minimum — never zero.

**The 14-day silence rule:** If two weeks pass with no post, copiers start dropping. Treat 14 days as the absolute ceiling between posts.

## Format Conventions

### Length
- Position thesis: 200-400 words
- Earnings reaction: 100-200 words
- Strategy post: 150-300 words
- Macro repurpose: as on X, trimmed of platform-specific phrasing

### Required Elements
- Specific tickers (use eToro's symbol linking)
- Position size disclosure when relevant ("This is ~8% of my portfolio")
- Conviction level ("High conviction" / "Starter position" / "Tactical")
- What would make you change your mind (especially on thesis posts)

### Avoid
- Price targets (compliance gray zone, and they age badly)
- "This is not financial advice" (eToro handles compliance disclaimer; over-disclaiming reads weak)
- Excessive hedging language ("might", "could", "possibly" in chains)

## Weekly Portfolio News Scan

A forcing function for Pillars 1 and 2. Run every Sunday evening or Monday before market open.

### Sources (per holding)
1. Company IR page (press releases)
2. Google News, last 7 days, filtered to the ticker + company name
3. SEC EDGAR — 8-K filings in the last week
4. Earnings calendar for the next 2 weeks (so you know what's coming)
5. Major analyst rating changes (TipRanks, MarketWatch, Seeking Alpha headlines)

### Triage Output (one line per holding)
| Holding | Status | Action |
|---------|--------|--------|
| AAPL | Post-worthy | Earnings 5/2 — schedule reaction post |
| MSFT | Monitor | Analyst upgrade, no thesis change — skip |
| NVDA | Nothing | No material news this week |
| TSLA | Post-worthy | CFO departure — strategy post on management risk |

### Filter for "post-worthy"
- Material to thesis (yes → post)
- Earnings within 14 days (yes → schedule post)
- News that copiers will see and panic about even if you're not panicking (yes → post a calming/clarifying note)
- Routine analyst noise (no → skip)
- Sector news without direct company impact (no → skip, or fold into macro post)

### Triage discipline
Output 3 columns: **Post**, **Monitor**, **Nothing**. The discipline is to keep "Nothing" entries — not every holding needs a post every week. The scan is a filter, not a content-generation mandate.

## What NOT to Post

- **Predictions with specific price targets** — they age badly and erode credibility on the bad ones; on the good ones they make you look lucky, not skilled.
- **Subtweets of other Popular Investors** — small ecosystem, reputation compounds either way.
- **Tease posts** — "I'm watching something interesting" without showing your hand. eToro is a transparency platform; teases work against the format.
- **Crypto/leveraged content** — if the underlying portfolio is long-only equities, don't dilute the brand. Copiers who want crypto are the wrong copiers for this portfolio.
- **Returns claims you don't have** — eToro displays your real returns next to your posts. Mismatch = instant credibility loss.
- **Generic motivational content** — "diamond hands", "stay the course" without substance. There's no shortage of this on eToro; it doesn't differentiate.
- **Recommendations** ("Buy X") — even if eToro allows it, framing as "I bought X and here's why" travels better and respects the "I trade my book publicly" frame.

## Voice on eToro

The voice is a tighter, more disclosure-forward version of the X voice. Less paradox-framing, more "here is what I'm doing and why" directness. Copiers want clarity, not cleverness.

- Direct: state the position, the size, the reason. No build-up.
- Honest about losses: drawdown posts build more trust than win posts. Don't hide.
- Specific: every claim has a number behind it.
- No FinTwit slang, no emojis, no "to the moon".
- Conversational where strategy posts allow — copiers want a person, not a research note.

See → [voice/brand-voice.md](../voice/brand-voice.md) for the underlying voice. The eToro adaptation strips out the longer literary register and leans practical.

## 90-Day Trial Frame

Before committing long-term, treat the first 90 days as a test:
- Goal: prove the content cadence is sustainable AND copier count moves at all
- Decision criteria at day 90:
  - Cadence sustained (>10 posts in 90 days): continue
  - Cadence broken (<8 posts): kill — won't scale to Elite anyway
  - Cadence sustained AND copier count moved (>5 copiers): scale up to 3x/week
  - Cadence sustained AND zero copier movement: diagnose (Risk Score? returns? content quality?) before continuing
