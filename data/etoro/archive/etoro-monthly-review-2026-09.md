- $SPX500: The S&P 500 closed September 2026 at 7,651.54, marking a monthly return of -0.45% from its August 31 close of 7,686.14. The index experienced an intra-month high of 7,782.19 on September 22 and a low of 7,507.77 on September 16.

- September 30: The August Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index, the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, showed lower-than-expected growth, easing some fears of an immediate October rate hike.

## Held Names That Reported / Moved Materially
- **$AMD:** The company saw a strong month, launching a new AI infrastructure platform in Saudi Arabia with Cisco and HUMAIN using its Instinct MI355X GPUs and EPYC CPUs. Its CFO projected the total addressable market could reach $3 trillion by 2030, a substantial increase from previous estimates. AMD also partnered with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure for MI400 series GPU deployments valued at $1.5 billion over three years. The stock hit a new all-time high and surpassed a $1 trillion market capitalization on September 21, fueled by strong AI demand and confirmed major contracts.
- **$TSM:** Taiwan Semiconductor reaffirmed its critical role in the AI buildout with a commitment to an additional $100 billion investment in the US, expanding its Phoenix footprint with new fabs, packaging plants, and an R&D center. The company reported robust August 2026 revenue, up 53.3% year-on-year, driven by strong AI demand, and saw MediaTek roll out its first 2-nanometer processor built on TSMC's process.
- **$GOOG:** Google Cloud launched new AI-powered supply chain solutions and integrated advanced generative AI capabilities across Workspace and Cloud, aimed at boosting enterprise productivity. A new cloud venture, Crux AI (Blackstone/Alphabet), secured a $22 billion chip loan specifically for Google Tensor Processing Unit (TPU) purchases. Google Cloud also announced an expanded strategic partnership with a major global retailer for core system migration and AI integration.
- **$BTC:** Bitcoin faced a hard fork early in the month, introducing a new mining algorithm. Despite initial volatility, it later saw significant institutional interest, including a nearly $1 billion single-day inflow into US spot Bitcoin ETFs. Fidelity's Jurrien Timmer also publicly pivoted bullish on Bitcoin, targeting $100,000.
- **$ETH:** The Ethereum network saw a collapse in transaction fees by over 85%, coinciding with a price rebound, increased whale activity, and an all-time high in the number of Ethereum wallets. The Glamsterdam upgrade, targeting faster transaction processing and enhanced sustainability, entered its final testnet phase with a mainnet launch expected in Q4 2026.
- **$PLTR:** Palantir strengthened its commercial and government AI thesis with several key developments. It appointed former AIG CEO Peter Zaffino as its Global Head of Financial Services, signaling a deeper push into the complex financial sector. An expanded alliance with PwC US was announced to help organizations scale enterprise AI. The US Army awarded Palantir a $48.1 million contract for ammunition management software. Furthermore, its sovereign AI thesis was reinforced through partnerships with NVIDIA, Nebius, and Fujitsu, including NVIDIA deploying a joint sovereign AI stack using Palantir's platforms.
- **$BABA:** Alibaba Cloud announced an ambitious global infrastructure expansion plan and new AI capabilities at its Apsara Conference, including new cloud regions and data center expansions in key international markets.
- **$AMZN:** The company faced a lawsuit from the FTC alleging overcharging of advertisers and manipulation of ad auctions. A tragic cargo plane incident also occurred in Miami, raising operational safety concerns. Counteracting these, Amazon announced a significant $5 billion investment to expand its North American logistics and fulfillment network and a $1 increase to its minimum starting pay for US core operations workers to $20/hour.
- **$IREN:** Iris Energy secured a substantial $2.4 billion GPU financing package from Blue Owl to acquire NVIDIA Blackwell Ultra GPUs, significantly expanding its AI cloud capacity. JPMorgan upgraded the stock to Overweight, raising its price target and citing momentum as a "neocloud" provider, its NVIDIA partnership, and a raised 2026 Annual Recurring Revenue guidance to $4.0 billion.
- **$TSLA:** Tesla launched its Cybercab robotaxi for initial deployment in Austin, a key step for its autonomous driving optionality. It also received regulatory approval in Germany for limited public testing of its Full Self-Driving (FSD) Beta software. The company secured $30 billion in new credit facilities to fund its aggressive expansion into AI computing infrastructure, solar cell production, and a semiconductor collaboration with SpaceX.
- **$HOOD:** Robinhood received an upgrade from Morgan Stanley to 'Overweight,' with a raised price target, citing improved economics from its expanded product suite. The company selected OG.com as its infrastructure and clearing partner for its Prediction Markets offering, which began rolling out in September. At its HOOD Summit 2026, Robinhood unveiled plans for crypto perpetual futures, 24/7 weekend equity trading, extended options hours, and new AI trading agents.
- **$CIFR:** Cipher Digital received conditional grid classifications from ERCOT covering 3.2 gigawatts of data center capacity, a crucial step for its AI colocation pivot. The company further solidified its shift to high-performance computing by extending its Barber Lake data center lease to 20 years with a leading AI lab, expected to increase total contracted revenue from $3.8 billion to over $9 billion.
- **$NVDA:** NVIDIA partnered with Toyota to integrate its DRIVE Thor platform into future autonomous vehicle fleets. It announced the next-generation "Blackwell Ultra" AI GPU, slated for a late 2027 release, underscoring its innovation pace. The company also increased its share repurchase authorization by $150 billion, bringing the total to an unprecedented $235 billion.
- **$EOSE:** Eos Energy partnered with Google and MN8 Energy on a $350 million clean energy development for Google's data centers, featuring Eos's Z3 zinc-based long-duration storage. The company also received an $87 million advance under the second tranche of its U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) loan, funding its manufacturing capacity expansion.
- **$META:** Meta Platforms' shares surged over 11% on September 21, adding $200 billion in market value, after its Muse personal AI agent rocketed to the top of the U.S. iOS free app chart as the fastest-growing AI application. The company also reportedly agreed to an $18 billion legal settlement to resolve claims regarding its platforms' impact on teen mental health.
- **$NFLX:** Netflix raised U.K. prices and launched its first e-commerce partnership in Southeast Asia with Shopee. However, it also faced a lawsuit from the Florida Attorney General and a Wells Fargo downgrade due to "worrying" engagement trends and concerns about its content slate.
