Hi Everyone,

Alibaba ($BABA), representing about 4.72% of our portfolio, reached a notable settlement yesterday with the U.S. government. The allegations covered a period from January 2016 to December 2024, involving a failure to stop roughly 80,000 product sales that violated federal laws. Resolving such disputes provides greater clarity and can remove a source of uncertainty that often weighs on investor sentiment, particularly for Chinese technology companies operating in international markets.

Alibaba has stated that a mutually satisfactory resolution was reached to bring stricter compliance to sales on its platforms. This event, while a negative headline, moves the company past a protracted legal issue. We view the removal of this regulatory uncertainty as a constructive step, allowing the market to focus more clearly on Alibaba's core business performance and its ongoing "China re-rating" thesis.
