This suggests underlying demand from informed capital, which views short-term weakness as an opportunity to build long-term positions.

Today, Devcon 8 opens in Mumbai, bringing together the Ethereum developer community. While this conference is crucial for the network's long-term development, history shows it rarely translates into immediate price movements. Our thesis for $ETH is not based on short-term catalysts but on its long-term role in the compute demand megatrend, which consumes the same primitives as AI.

We are holding our $ETH position at its current size. My long-term view remains unchanged: we will continue to hold through price volatility as long as the underlying thesis of Ethereum's role in the future of decentralized compute remains intact.
