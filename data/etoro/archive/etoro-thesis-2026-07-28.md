## Draft Tuesday Post

Hi Everyone,

I know it can be frustrating to see $ETH continue to sit in drawdown, currently -29.9% on our cost basis at 12.59% of the portfolio. However, recent developments suggest the market narrative may be shifting, and I continue to hold conviction in its long-term trajectory.

After months of soft flows, Ethereum ETFs saw significant inflows of $103.9 million in the week of July 20-24, outpacing Bitcoin funds by nearly three times. This marks their third consecutive week of net inflows, totaling close to $294 million over the past three weeks. This renewed institutional interest helped push $ETH to a two-month high above $1,970, with the asset gaining 5% last week. Even more tellingly, the $ETH/$BTC ratio broke a year-long downtrend in July, a technical signal that some, like Fundstrat's Tom Lee, are interpreting as the beginning of a broader crypto rotation.

While concerns about network usage and supply growth have persisted, the upcoming Glamsterdam upgrade, slated for the second half of 2026, is specifically designed to tackle these issues. This major upgrade aims to cut gas costs by 78% and boost throughput towards 10,000 transactions per second, directly addressing the mainnet activity challenges we have observed. We are also seeing strong signs of institutional adoption, with firms like Bitmine Immersion Technologies (BMNR) accumulating 5.79 million ETH and actively staking over 4.9 million tokens. Combined with ongoing regulatory clarity efforts in the US and Europe, the fundamental rails for Ethereum's growth are becoming more robust.

We will continue to hold $ETH at its current weight, allowing the thesis to play out as these institutional flows and network enhancements gain momentum.
