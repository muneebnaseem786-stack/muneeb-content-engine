## Draft Tuesday Post

Hi Everyone,

$BABA (Alibaba) currently sits at around 6.09% of our portfolio and is in a small drawdown of -1.98% on our cost basis. Our thesis on Alibaba continues to be rooted in the long-term China re-rating story, a complex narrative that reflects both domestic strengths and geopolitical realities.

We continue to hold $BABA because we believe the company is taking concrete steps to streamline its operations and focus on its most valuable segments. The recent decision to divest its non-core gaming unit, Lingxi Interactive Entertainment, highlights a strategic pivot towards core e-commerce and high-growth AI cloud services. Analysts are viewing this positively, and it aligns with our belief that a more focused Alibaba can unlock greater value. Furthermore, the company's consistent share buybacks, including the 1.055 million shares repurchased on June 29, signal management's confidence in its future and commitment to shareholder returns. Independent valuations also suggest $BABA is currently undervalued by almost 20% against its intrinsic value.

I acknowledge the significant external pressures facing Chinese companies. While China's factory activity expanded in June, signaling some economic momentum, domestic demand remains sluggish, and new outbound investment regulations linking overseas expansion to national security are set to take effect. Alibaba also faces ongoing US regulatory scrutiny, including a lawsuit against the Department of Defense over its "Chinese military company" designation, and recent allegations regarding its AI lab. These are real challenges that introduce volatility.

However, we believe the long-term re-rating potential remains intact as Alibaba focuses on its core strengths and navigates the evolving regulatory and geopolitical landscape. We will continue to monitor China's domestic consumption trends and the implications of the new investment regulations closely.
