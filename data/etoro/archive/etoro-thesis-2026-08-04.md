Hi Everyone,

$ETH remains our largest position in drawdown by invested capital, currently sitting at 12.59% of the portfolio and down -30.5% on our cost basis. I appreciate that seeing our biggest position showing a significant paper loss can feel frustrating, especially during broader market volatility. However, we continue to hold $ETH with conviction, as the underlying thesis remains strong.

Despite recent price softness, institutional demand for Ethereum has shown clear signs of strengthening. July recorded $365 million in net inflows into US spot Ethereum ETFs, marking the strongest monthly performance of 2026 and notably outpacing Bitcoin ETF flows. This influx of capital reflects growing institutional appetite for the asset. This was further highlighted by Morgan Stanley launching new Ethereum ETFs in late July. We are also seeing significant whale accumulation, with Bitmine Immersion Technologies adding another 150,120 ETH to its staking holdings in early August, bringing their total to over 5 million ETH, or nearly 5% of the circulating supply. These are not speculative short-term moves; they are structural positioning.

Ethereum's long-term thesis is tied to its role as the foundational layer for decentralized finance, NFTs, and a growing ecosystem of Layer 2 networks. While its price has not yet reflected its fundamental activity, the network continues to show robust growth in staking and stablecoin activity. The ETH/BTC ratio's recent climb to a three-month high of 0.030 further reinforces Ethereum's relative strength and its critical place in the crypto landscape.

We acknowledge that the Glamsterdam network upgrade, a key development for scaling, has seen a delay into Q3 2026. This type of iterative development is normal for complex blockchain technology, and we view it as a necessary step to ensure robust implementation rather than a thesis-breaking event. Early August also saw a modest $11.9 million net outflow from US spot Ethereum ETFs, indicating some short-term cooling. We continue to monitor macro headwinds, as tightening liquidity and rising Treasury yields can impact crypto assets.

Our conviction remains that Ethereum is essential infrastructure for the future of finance and the digital economy. We will hold our $ETH position at current weight, and I expect operating performance and continued institutional adoption to ultimately close the gap on our cost basis over the next two to three years.
