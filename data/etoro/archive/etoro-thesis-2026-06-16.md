## Draft Tuesday Post

Hi Everyone,

Our portfolio holds two key positions in the crypto mining sector, $IREN (Iris Energy) and $CIFR (Cipher Digital). While both generate revenue from Bitcoin mining, our thesis is increasingly focused on their strategic pivot to high-performance computing (HPC) and AI data center infrastructure. We currently hold $IREN at 2.48% invested (+113.06% P/L) and $CIFR at 1.25% invested (+162.29% P/L).

The core of the thesis for both companies is their ability to leverage existing energy infrastructure and expertise in data center operations to capture demand from the rapidly expanding AI compute market. $IREN, in particular, has made significant strides in this direction. Just this week, on June 15, 2026, the company completed the acquisition of Nostrum Group, expanding its AI Cloud platform into Europe. This follows their announcement on June 3, 2026, of a new 800MW data center campus in South Australia and the closing of a $3.65 billion investment-grade GPU financing facility on June 1, 2026, backed by Microsoft for an AI Cloud contract. $IREN also secured a substantial $3.4 billion AI Cloud contract with NVIDIA on May 7, 2026. While one analyst recently lowered estimates due to a delayed AI revenue ramp, we believe these recent developments strongly validate the long-term AI infrastructure pivot.

$CIFR is pursuing a similar path, building industrial-scale data centers for HPC workloads. On June 15, 2026, Cipher Digital priced an $810 million offering of senior secured notes, which closed the same day. This financing is intended to fund the completion of their Stingray Facility and support ongoing expansion plans. Although Q1 2026 saw a wider loss, the company has consistently highlighted its strategic shift towards HPC and AI data contracts. Insider selling reported in early June is something we monitor, but it does not change the underlying conviction in the strategic direction and funding for growth.

From a Bitcoin mining perspective, the recent 10.09% drop in Bitcoin mining difficulty on June 14, 2026, is a tailwind. This significant adjustment, driven by a ~15% Bitcoin price correction in June, means remaining miners are operating with improved economics. This provides a more favourable environment for the Bitcoin-derived revenue streams of both $IREN and $CIFR as they continue their transition to diversified AI infrastructure providers. We are holding both positions, confident in their dual strategy of leveraging Bitcoin mining cash flow while aggressively building out AI compute capacity.
