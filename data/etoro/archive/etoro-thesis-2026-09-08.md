EIP-8141 is particularly interesting as it would allow users to pay transaction fees in stablecoins instead of $ETH, enhancing user flexibility.

These developments reinforce our long-term conviction in Ethereum as a foundational layer for the decentralized future, driven by continuous innovation, increasing institutional acceptance, and a clear roadmap for scalability and security. The current committed capital to the network, with approximately 42.9 million $ETH staked across nearly a million validators, further strengthens this view. We will continue to hold our position, letting the compounding network effects and ongoing development close the gap on our cost basis over the coming years.
