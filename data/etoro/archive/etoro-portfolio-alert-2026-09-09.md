```post
Hi Everyone,

We have important news for $EOSE (Eos Energy Enterprises), one of our thematic tail bets in the energy storage sector, which currently represents 0.88% of our portfolio value. Eos is also consolidating battery manufacturing, targeting 10-15% lower conversion costs from 2027 and approximately 4 GWh of capacity, which should improve profitability as these new projects come online. We believe this development positions Eos well for future growth in the rapidly expanding energy storage market.
```
