I know it can feel frustrating when our crypto-related positions like $COIN and $HOOD are in drawdown. $HOOD is currently around 3.4% of the portfolio at -18.94% on our cost, while $COIN sits at about 3.0% with a -28.05% P/L. However, the long-term thesis for these "crypto rails" remains firmly in place, and we are seeing significant strategic execution from both companies despite the market headwinds. Robinhood, for example, just launched its public mainnet for Robinhood Chain, an Arbitrum-based Layer 2 blockchain designed for tokenized real-world assets and DeFi applications. They also introduced decentralized lending with Robinhood Earn, new stock tokens, and expanded their Agentic Trading capabilities to crypto, alongside plans for broader international expansion.

Coinbase has been equally aggressive in building out its platform. Their recent "System Update" unveiled 21 new products, including a unified global liquidity pool for derivatives, Bitcoin-backed mortgages, AI-powered advisory services, and tokenized US stocks for eligible non-US customers. They are now the first exchange approved by the CFTC to offer global regulated crypto derivatives to US customers. This push to diversify revenue streams beyond volatile spot trading, into derivatives, subscriptions, and new financial products, is crucial for their long-term growth and resilience.

These moves are not just incremental updates; they represent foundational investments in the infrastructure that will power the next wave of crypto adoption. While the market continues to grapple with macro pressures and regulatory uncertainty, both $COIN and $HOOD are diligently building the rails for a more integrated and accessible crypto financial system. We will continue to hold these positions, confident that their strategic expansion will be rewarded as the crypto cycle matures.
