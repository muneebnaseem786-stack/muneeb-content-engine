Hi Everyone,

The week ahead brings a significant macro catalyst with the Federal Open Market Committee ($FOMC) meeting on June 16-17. We expect an interest rate decision, but the market will be keenly watching the accompanying statement and remarks from the new Fed Chair, Kevin Warsh, for any shift from an easing bias to a more hawkish stance, especially as Fed Funds futures are pricing in potential rate hikes for 2026. US retail sales figures are also due on Wednesday, June 17, providing another key read on consumer health.

Our AI compute cluster saw substantial developments this past week. $AMD announced a £2 billion investment in UK AI innovation and research, which I see as a strong long-term commitment. Major Wall Street analysts also raised price targets for $AMD, reinforcing its role as a leading alternative in the AI chip market. In the AI platform space, $GOOG is seeing AI demand exceeding supply, justifying its aggressive CapEx plans for 2026.

In the crypto sector, it was a volatile week. Both $BTC and $ETH experienced significant price drops, with $BTC falling below $60,000 before a tentative recovery. Record ETF outflows since mid-May and hawkish Fed expectations are weighing heavily on sentiment, and we continue to hold our positions with conviction, viewing current volatility as part of the market cycle. $GLXY, a key component of our crypto rails exposure, surged after announcing a new institutional partnership with Morgan Stanley Wealth Management, a clear positive for traditional finance integration. Elsewhere, $TAO also saw a notable surge, breaking a seven-month downtrend, fueled by news of Anthropic AI model bans that could benefit decentralized alternatives. $CIFR announced pricing of $810 million in senior secured notes to fund facility completion, a key step in its AI data center pivot.

Geopolitical factors also played a role, with $BABA shares declining for seven consecutive days after being classified by the US Department of Defense as a Chinese military entity, and facing regulatory scrutiny from Beijing. I will continue to monitor this for our China re-rating cluster.

With the US markets closed on Friday, June 19, for Juneteenth, expect focus to remain on the FOMC outcome and retail sales data earlier in the week.
