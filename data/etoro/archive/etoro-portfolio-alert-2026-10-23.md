Hi Everyone,

SoFi Technologies ($SOFI), which makes up 0.86% of our portfolio, made a significant move yesterday by launching "SoFi Earn," or $SOFIE. This product directly addresses the issue of idle capital within the digital asset ecosystem, a point highlighted by SoFi CEO Anthony Noto. We believe this innovation significantly strengthens $SOFI's position in bridging traditional banking with the digital asset space and reinforces our thesis around the company's "crypto rails" strategy. It expands their regulated digital asset offerings, making SoFi a key player in how consumers interact with crypto through a traditional financial institution. We will be watching for adoption rates and how this new product integrates into their broader ecosystem as it rolls out through November.
