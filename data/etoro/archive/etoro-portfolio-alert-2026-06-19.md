Hi Everyone,

Furthermore, major AI customers are already committed to Trainium capacity through AWS, highlighting the demand.

We will be closely monitoring how this strategy develops. If Amazon successfully establishes itself as a significant external supplier of AI chips, it further validates the massive, ongoing buildout in AI compute infrastructure, a core thesis across our portfolio. This also offers another avenue for growth within our existing $AMZN position as they broaden their participation in the AI value chain.
