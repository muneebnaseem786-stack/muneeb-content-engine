Hi Everyone,

$ETH is currently around 7.90% of our portfolio by value, reflecting an invested allocation of 12.74% which is in a drawdown of -21.60%. I know it can feel frustrating when a high-conviction position is in the red, but our long-term thesis remains firmly intact and is supported by ongoing fundamental developments.

Our core belief in $ETH is as the foundational layer for decentralized compute ,  the "world computer" that underpins the next generation of financial infrastructure and digital applications. The upcoming Glamsterdam hard fork, still targeted for the first half of 2026, is a significant step towards realizing this vision. It represents a fundamental architectural shift to scale Layer 1, aiming to improve censorship resistance, enable parallel processing, and potentially triple network throughput. We anticipate this upgrade will lead to a substantial increase in transactions per second and a projected 78% reduction in fees, making Ethereum even more attractive for developers and users.

While $ETH experienced significant ETF outflows in May, which saw a record 17-day streak of withdrawals, we also witnessed a positive shift more recently, with $82.37 million in net inflows on June 8, 2026, led by Fidelity and BlackRock products. This volatility is part of the adoption curve for a nascent asset class, but the continued institutional interest, coupled with large strategic purchases like BitMine Immersion Technologies acquiring over 126,000 $ETH last week, reinforces the long-term demand for the network.

We continue to hold $ETH at its current weight, viewing the recent price action as market noise around a thesis that is actively unfolding. I expect the compounding effects of layer-1 scaling and sustained institutional adoption to reward patience as the Glamsterdam upgrade rolls out and its benefits become more apparent throughout 2026.
