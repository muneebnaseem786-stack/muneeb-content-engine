## Draft Tuesday Post

Hi Everyone,

Ethereum, represented by $ETH, currently stands as our largest position in drawdown, at 12.74% of the portfolio with a P/L of -21.60%. I appreciate that seeing this performance can be frustrating, especially as the broader crypto market navigates a challenging macro environment. $ETH has seen a significant pullback this month, trading around $2,307-$2,315 as of today, representing a roughly 13.2% decline for October. Technically, the price has closed below its critical 200-day moving average for four consecutive days, a clear signal of near-term weakness.

The current softness is tied to a mix of factors. Investor caution ahead of the US Federal Reserve's rate decision on October 27-28 is a primary macroeconomic driver, impacting risk assets across the board. We have also seen continued outflows from spot Ether ETFs, with $225 million shed in net outflows during the week of October 19 alone, marking the fourth consecutive week of this trend. This persistent institutional selling pressure naturally weighs on price.

Despite these headwinds, the core thesis for Ethereum's long-term utility remains intact. We see pockets of significant institutional interest. Bitmine Immersion Technologies ($BMNR) continued its targeted accumulation, adding over 10,000 $ETH in the past week and nearing its self-imposed 5% ownership cap. Critically, JPMorgan Chase recently launched its JPM Coin deposit token directly on the Ethereum blockchain, a clear signal of growing adoption for institutional finance on public networks.

On the protocol front, the "Glamsterdam" upgrade's activation on the Hoodi testnet, initially scheduled for October 26, was postponed due to smart-contract gas risks. While this creates some uncertainty around the mainnet rollout timeline, developers did merge a fix for a validator deposit flaw related to the upgrade on October 26, demonstrating continued progress. We are watching these developments closely, as successful scaling remains key for Ethereum's future. The shift of activity to Layer 2 networks has, however, meant that $ETH's circulating supply has grown over the past month as the burn mechanism has become less effective.

We will continue to hold our $ETH position at its current weight. I will be watching for the outcome of the Federal Reserve's decision, any clarity on the Glamsterdam mainnet timeline, and a reversal in ETF flow trends as catalysts that could shift the near-term outlook.
