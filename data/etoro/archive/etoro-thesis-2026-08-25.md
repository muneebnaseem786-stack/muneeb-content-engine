$ETH is currently our portfolio's largest position in drawdown, making up about 12.6% of the portfolio with a -7.0% P/L. Ethereum saw a strong rally on August 19-20, with the price surging to an intraday peak near $2,300, and it has recently held around $2,500. This move was supported by a broader shift in risk appetite following the US Treasury's increased bond buybacks and a significant short squeeze in the crypto market.

Crucially for our thesis, institutional interest in Ethereum continues to accelerate. Spot $ETH ETFs recorded approximately $697 million in net inflows in the week to August 21, followed by another $116 million on August 24, marking six consecutive days of positive flows. Total ETF net assets now stand at nearly $147.4 billion. This is a material shift from the earlier part of the year and suggests growing institutional acceptance. We are also seeing major players like Fidelity, Morgan Stanley, and BlackRock expanding their Ethereum-related institutional offerings, from staking funds to tokenizing money market funds directly on the Ethereum blockchain.

We continue to view $ETH through the lens of compute demand and institutional rails, integrating it into our equity book with the same long-term hold discipline as our other high-conviction positions. While market dynamics will always bring volatility, these deepening layers of institutional adoption and the ongoing network developments like the proposed Hegotá upgrade and discussions around making $ETH more deflationary, strengthen our view on its foundational role in the digital economy. We remain comfortable holding $ETH at its current weight, allowing the thesis to play out over the coming quarters and years.
