Hi Everyone,

Our $ETH position, currently 12.60% of the portfolio by invested capital, has been a frustrating hold, sitting around -30% on its cost basis. Despite this, the underlying thesis around Ethereum's role as the foundation for the smart contract economy and its critical compute demand remains strong.

July brought a much-needed rebound for $ETH, with the cryptocurrency posting 20.3% gains for the month, its strongest performance in a year. This helped claw back a significant portion of June's decline. The price has firmed up around $1,900 in early August, partly benefiting from a weaker-than-expected July jobs report that cooled expectations for further Fed rate hikes, a positive for risk assets like crypto. Spot $ETH ETF demand also contributed to July's recovery, with net inflows observed on most trading days.

However, the on-chain data presents a mixed picture. While active network addresses have seen a notable decline since earlier this year, suggesting reduced user engagement, we are also seeing significant accumulation from larger holders. Whale addresses holding 1,000 to 10,000 $ETH spiked in late June, coinciding with lower prices, which historically indicates strategic positioning by those with deep conviction. This divergence between falling network usage and increasing institutional interest is a key dynamic we are watching.

The long-term thesis for $ETH is rooted in its continuous development as the leading platform for decentralised finance ($DeFi), Layer-2 scaling solutions, and the growing tokenisation of real-world assets. These applications are critical drivers of on-chain compute demand, aligning directly with our broader portfolio's barbell bet on AI and crypto megatrends. While recent proposals from developers regarding staking rewards may introduce some debate around institutional demand, the core utility and ecosystem strength of Ethereum continue to grow.

We will continue to hold our $ETH position at its current weight. What would change our stance is any material reversal in the development roadmap, a sustained breakdown in its Layer-2 ecosystem, or a significant regulatory shift that fundamentally undermines its utility as a compute platform.
