## Option 2: $COIN
**Catalyst (last 24h):** Coinbase received Financial Services Permission from the FSRA in Abu Dhabi to establish its international tokenization hub.
**Source:** https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/coinbase-establishes-its-tokenization-hub-in-abu-dhabi-with-financial-services-permission-from-the-financial-services-regulatory-authority-fsra-302302194.html
**Why this works as a post:** Regulatory approval for a new, strategic business line in a key financial hub directly impacts Coinbase's long-term crypto rails thesis.

```post
Hi Everyone,

Coinbase, a core holding in our crypto rails cluster (2.62% of the portfolio), announced a significant strategic development today. The company has received Financial Services Permission from the Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) in Abu Dhabi to establish its international tokenization hub in the Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM). This is a crucial step for Coinbase, positioning it to build infrastructure for a more open and accessible global financial system through tokenized securities.

This license allows Coinbase to arrange deals in investments and provide custody to facilitate the launch of tokenized securities. The company emphasizes that this initiative can address the 4 billion people worldwide who currently lack access to capital markets, by offering tokenized securities that are fully backed by underlying shares and provide full shareholder rights, including dividends and voting. This move leverages Abu Dhabi's progressive regulatory framework for virtual assets, which treats tokenized equities as securities, blockchain-native tokens, and DeFi-composable assets simultaneously. It is a clear validation of the long-term thesis for the tokenization of real-world assets and the expansion of crypto's role in traditional finance.

We view this as a material development for $COIN, solidifying its position as a global financial infrastructure platform. It expands the addressable market for the company significantly and strengthens our conviction in its ability to drive institutional adoption and innovation within the broader crypto ecosystem.
```
