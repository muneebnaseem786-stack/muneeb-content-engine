Hi Everyone,

The week ahead brings a heavy calendar of US economic data and some notable corporate updates. On Wednesday, we get the US Consumer Price Index ($CPI) for July, followed by the Producer Price Index ($PPI) on Thursday, and July retail sales data on Friday. These inflation and spending prints will be key for understanding the Federal Reserve's path and broader market sentiment.

Our portfolio saw a lot of individual company news flow this past week. In crypto, both $BTC and $ETH experienced volatility. $BTC climbed after Jim Cramer's announcement to sell, illustrating the "Inverse Cramer" effect, though hardware wallet security concerns and August's historical weakness also featured. $ETH saw institutional inflows and outperformed the Nasdaq 100 in July, and our position in $BMNR (Bitmine Immersion Technologies) continued to aggressively accumulate and stake $ETH, reporting significant share buybacks and Russell 1000 index inclusion.

In our AI compute cluster, $AMZN posted strong Q2 results, driven by accelerating AWS growth and a lifted FY26 capital expenditure plan for AI and data centers. News of Jeff Bezos selling shares, pre-planned under a Rule 10b5-1, led to some overnight selling, but the underlying business remains robust. $BABA unveiled its latest AI model, Qianwen 3.8-Max, with an aggressive pricing strategy and plans for an open-source release, reinforcing its AI ambitions despite ongoing legal scrutiny. $TAO (Bittensor) saw increased institutional confidence from Grayscale and new payment integration with AEON, strengthening its utility within the AI ecosystem.

$CIFR (Cipher Digital) continued its strategic pivot from Bitcoin mining to high-performance computing data centers, announcing new site acquisitions and a significant bond offering, though its Q2 earnings missed expectations. Our tail bet in $TE (T1 Energy) jumped on a major solar contract win and also announced Q2 earnings for this Wednesday, August 12. Meanwhile, $SOFI reported a Q2 earnings beat, but its stock dipped as the market focused on flat profit guidance and balance sheet concerns, even as it secured a new partnership with Notre Dame Athletics. Lastly, $FUTU will report Q2 earnings later in August, but it continues to face ongoing securities fraud class actions.

I will provide an update later in the week if the inflation or retail sales data materially shifts our outlook or if there are any significant developments from $TE's earnings.
