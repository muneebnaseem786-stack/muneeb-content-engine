## Option 1: $NVDA
**Catalyst (last 24h):** NVIDIA delayed the launch of its Kyber rack-scale architecture and the 2027 Rubin Ultra chips to 2028 due to manufacturing challenges with a critical circuit board.
**Source:** https://www.gurufocus.com/news/2475765/nvidia-nvda-delays-launch-of-kyber-rackscale-architecture-to-2028
**Why this works as a post:** This is a material operational setback for a core AI compute holding, impacting their next-generation product roadmap.

```post
Hi Everyone,

A significant development has emerged for $NVDA today, impacting their aggressive roadmap in AI compute. The company has announced a delay in the rollout of its Kyber rack-scale architecture, which was designed to support the upcoming 2027 Rubin Ultra chips. This crucial next-generation system is now postponed to 2028, a delay of over a year.

The delay stems from manufacturing challenges associated with a critical circuit board, specifically the midplane PCB, which is a core component for these advanced AI servers. Additionally, plans for a backup configuration were scrapped following pushback from cloud service providers regarding its design and operational complexity. This highlights potential vulnerabilities in NVIDIA's supply chain and manufacturing processes, which could impact their ability to meet the growing demand for AI solutions, particularly as the annual release cadence collides with manufacturing limits.

Our position in $NVDA sits at around 0.92% of the portfolio. While this delay is a setback for the bleeding edge of their roadmap, it is important to remember that current-generation Rubin systems are still set to ship this fall to various cloud partners, including Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. We will be watching for further commentary on how this delay might affect near-term competitive dynamics and customer adoption of future platforms. I expect the market to digest this news carefully, but our long-term thesis on NVIDIA as a foundational player in AI compute remains intact as demand for AI hardware continues to be robust.
```

## Option 2: $BABA
**Catalyst (last 24h):** A federal judge ordered the Pentagon to grant Alibaba a temporary reprieve from a US law that had caused its lobbyists to sever ties, while the court considers the law's constitutionality.
**Source:** https://www.straitstimes.com/business/alibaba-gets-reprieve-on-lobbying-ban-tied-to-us-blacklist
**Why this works as a post:** This is a material regulatory development that could reduce friction for a key China re-rating holding.

```post
Hi Everyone,

Some welcome news emerged over the weekend for $BABA, our holding in the China re-rating cluster that currently makes up about 4.72% of the portfolio. A federal judge in the US has ordered the Pentagon to provide Alibaba Group Holding with a temporary reprieve from a law that previously caused all of its lobbyists to drop the company as a client. This order will stand while the judge considers the constitutionality of the measure itself, which is set to test the US's ability to curtail the activities of Chinese companies.

The restriction had barred the Defense Department from working with any company represented by lobbyists who also work for entities blacklisted by the Pentagon, forcing lobbying firms to choose. Alibaba argued that these restrictions violated its freedom of speech and led to a loss of its voice across its dealings with the federal government. This temporary block on the lobbying restriction is a positive step towards reducing regulatory headwinds and offers some clarity as the legal challenge progresses. It signals that the judicial system is actively scrutinizing the scope of these US blacklisting measures.

While Alibaba recently made internal strategic moves, such as banning employees from using Anthropic's Claude Code due to security concerns, this external regulatory reprieve is a more immediate and potentially impactful development for its operations in the US. We will continue to monitor the legal proceedings, as a permanent resolution could further improve the operating environment for Alibaba in the US market.
```
