Hi Everyone,

Our $ETH position currently sits at around 12.74% of the portfolio, and I appreciate that seeing it down about -21.60% on our cost basis can feel frustrating given the recent market dynamics. However, we continue to hold this position based on a thesis that prioritises long-term network development and institutional adoption over short-term price fluctuations.

A key part of our conviction lies in Ethereum's continuous evolution. Developers have scheduled the Glamsterdam network upgrade for the Hoodi testnet on October 26, 2026, which is a significant step towards boosting transaction capacity and improving block production. This upgrade, already successfully activated on the Sepolia testnet on October 6, is designed to more than triple the block gas limit to 200 million and introduces core protocol changes like enshrined proposer-builder separation. These are essential for improving Ethereum's efficiency and long-term scalability, even if a mainnet activation date is still being determined. Separately, Vitalik Buterin's recent EIP-8288 proposal, aimed at offloading complex cryptographic data to user devices, could also contribute to a leaner network and reduced verification costs, further enhancing efficiency.

While we acknowledge the recent headwinds ,  specifically the $320.1 million in outflows from U.S. spot Ethereum ETFs in the week of October 12, 16 and the broader crypto ETF outflows exceeding $1 billion this month ,  these market movements do not change the fundamental thesis for holding $ETH. We see the ongoing institutional buildout as a strong counter-narrative. J.P. Morgan's launch of JPM Coin on the Ethereum Layer 1 blockchain on October 19, 2026, is a prime example of traditional finance increasingly leveraging Ethereum for institutional payments and on-chain cash solutions. This significantly strengthens the argument for Ethereum as a foundational layer for digital finance, aligning with our broader crypto thesis that focuses on compute demand and infrastructure.

We will continue to hold our $ETH position at its current weight, allowing the long-term compounding effects of network improvements and institutional adoption to play out.
