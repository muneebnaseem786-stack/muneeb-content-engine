## Draft Tuesday Post

Dear investors,

The second pillar of our long-term conviction in $PLTR (Palantir) centers on the significant unlock of its commercial addressable market. The exceptional rigor and security requirements of serving the US government, particularly in defense and intelligence, have created a unique credential for Palantir. This proven track record of deploying AI proof-of-concepts to production at scale in the most complex environments directly translates into credibility within the private sector.

Enterprise CIOs are not making nine-figure AI deployment decisions lightly. They seek solutions that have demonstrated effectiveness where the cost of failure is immensely high, and Palantir's government work provides precisely that reference. This enables a meaningful compression in commercial selling cycles. The commercial segment is structurally much larger than the public-sector pie that historically anchored the company. Where US federal IT spend is constrained by appropriations, private sector AI infrastructure spend is primarily bounded by demonstrable return on investment.

The first quarter 2026 results, reported earlier in May, clearly illustrate this unlock. US commercial revenue surged by 133% year-on-year to $595 million. The US commercial customer count expanded to 615, a 42% increase year-on-year. Management subsequently raised the full-year US commercial guide to over $3.224 billion, indicating at least 120% growth. This is further reinforced by the closing of 206 deals valued at $1 million or more in the quarter, with 47 exceeding $10 million. These figures represent tangible bookings, not just pipeline narratives.

Furthermore, recent developments like the enhanced partnership with SAP for AI-supported data migration and the general availability of a new bring-your-own-model implementation across Palantir's AI Platform demonstrate ongoing product innovation and deepening commercial engagement. Our position in $PLTR remains around 6.87% of the portfolio, currently at -11.92% on our cost basis. I am comfortable allowing continued commercial operating performance to close this gap over the next two to three years. The third pillar of our $PLTR thesis, focusing on the allied-government layer, will be discussed in next Tuesday's note.

Thanks,
