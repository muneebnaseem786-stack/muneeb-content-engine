## Locked Strategy Framing

**"Contrarian holds, AI compute + crypto thesis, hold through thesis-not-price."**

The portfolio is a barbell bet on two megatrends that consume the same primitives (semis + energy): AI and crypto. The execution style is:

1. **Concentration over diversification.** Top 5 = 52%. Few names, deep conviction.
2. **Don't trim winners until thesis breaks.** AMD scaled from small to largest at +144%, not sold down.
3. **Don't cut losers because of price.** Drawdown alone isn't a sell signal; only changing fundamentals are. ETH -16% still biggest cost-basis position. PLTR -16% still 6.5% of book.
4. **Crypto inside the equity book.** Not a separate asset class — same thesis lens (compute demand), same hold discipline.

## Position Theses

### $PLTR Long-Term Thesis (locked 19 May 2026)

Three-pillar conviction frame. This is the locked long-term view; all $PLTR posts going forward should map back to one or more of these pillars.

1. **Only proven AI POC-to-production at scale.** $PLTR is the only company that has taken AI proof of concepts and moved them into production at meaningful scale across complex enterprise and government workflows. Most of the AI software space is still stuck at the pilot stage; Palantir is shipping.
2. **Commercial TAM dwarfs the public-sector pie.** Having proven the US government as a long-term customer across defence and intelligence, the credibility carry-over into the private sector is significant. The commercial addressable market is multiples larger than the public-sector pie that originally built the company.
3. **Allied-government layer not priced in.** Allied governments outside the US (UK, Israel, parts of NATO, and increasingly the Middle East) represent an additional demand layer that the market has not fully priced.

**Accumulation plan:** add between $128 and $130 to bring cost basis down on the existing -16% position. If price runs from here and that level does not print, hold existing 6.5% weight and let operating performance close the gap over the next 2-3 years.

**What would change the thesis:** any sign that POC-to-production scaling is stalling, a clear customer-count deceleration, or material reversal in US commercial growth. Drawdown alone is not a sell signal.
